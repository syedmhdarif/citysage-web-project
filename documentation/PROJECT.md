# Project List

## MD Analytics AI Copilot

IMAGE URL: https://citysage.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/1.png

CitySage enhanced MD Analytics 2.0 with the CitySage AI Copilot, a bilingual conversational agent built with LLMs, RAG, and hybrid connectors. It delivers fast, natural Q&A over KPIs, dashboards, and maps across eight urban use cases, significantly accelerating time-to-insight for city councils and laying the groundwork for actionable AI.

Service Type: Agentic AI Module
Client/Partner: Map2U


## KLUO RoadSense

IMAGE URL: https://citysage.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2.png

CitySage collaborated with Map2U to advance an Urban Observatory for a Klang Valley city council. Two AI solutions were delivered: a 3D AI Digital Twin for city planning and analytics, and a Road Asset AI Model for automated road asset detection and defect classification such as potholes.

Service Type: Consultation & AI Module Development
Client: City Council

## MUO – NSCP 2.0 Smart City Platform

IMAGE URL: https://citysage.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/3.png

Service Type: Agentic AI & Workflow Automation
Client Prime Contractor: Map2U
Beneficiaries: Ministries, State Agencies, PBTs

## JUPEM – MyCartoGo Enhancements

## SCOUT – JCorp Supply Chain Control Tower

IMAGE URL: https://citysage.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/5.png

SCOUT (Supply Chain Optimization & Unified Tech) is an Agentic AI-powered logistics control tower designed for Johor Corporation’s diverse business verticals. CitySage delivered the consulting and operational mockup (v0) showcasing real-time visibility, exception management, and unified supply chain optimization across Agribusiness, Healthcare, F&B, and Real Estate domains.

Service Type: Consultation & AI-Driven Supply Chain Transformation
Client: Johor Corporation
